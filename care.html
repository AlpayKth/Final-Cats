How to Care for Your Cat

Providing a healthy environment for your feline companion.

Feeding & Nutrition

Provide a balanced diet rich in animal protein. Always ensure a bowl of fresh, clean water is available at all times.

Grooming Needs

Regular brushing keeps the coat healthy and reduces shedding. Long-haired cats may need daily attention to prevent knots.

Litter Box Care

Keep the litter box in a quiet, accessible location. It must be scooped daily to ensure proper hygiene and cat comfort.

Exercise & Play

Cats need mental and physical stimulation. Use laser pointers, feather wands, and scratching posts to keep them active.

Important: Always schedule annual check-ups with a veterinarian to keep up with vaccinations and health checks.
